## Description (4,000 max)

Forgelings is a habit app that is gentle with you.

Each day, turn over a card with one small thing to do: give one task your full attention, reach out to someone, fix one small annoyance for good. Do it, and a new Forgeling joins your binder. Every one is a pixel creature drawn just for that moment, from ten realms, each with its own look, stats and story.

MISS A DAY? NOTHING IS LOST.
There are no streaks to break. Rest days are always fine. Your progress never resets, and nothing is ever taken away.

A HEARTH TO COME BACK TO
Your favourite Forgelings sit by a small fire. Give them a snack, tell them a story, or play one of five little games together. Send one out to wander, and they come back with a curio for your shelf. The one by the fire notices the days you show up, and every so often brings back something you wrote a while ago.

A MAP THAT DRAWS ITSELF
The world starts as blank paper. Each Forgeling you find inks in more of its realm, with places to uncover, trails between them, and old tales to piece back together, one curio at a time.

KIND BY DESIGN
- Nothing is for sale. Every Forgeling and every pack is earned by showing up, and the odds are shown in the app.
- No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone.
- One gentle reminder a day, only if you want it, and only on days your card is still waiting.
- Send a curio to a friend as a gift.

Forgelings is a small, independent app, shaped by a group of early testers. Thank you for trying it.
