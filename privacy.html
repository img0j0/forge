Forge

Privacy policy

Last updated October 20, 2026

Short version: Forge collects nothing. Everything you do in Forge stays on your device, and nobody else can see it, including the people who make Forge.

What Forge keeps, and where

Forge keeps your Forgelings, the days you showed up or rested, anything you write (names, tales, margin notes and feedback answers), your settings, and the fact that you are an adult. Your birth date is never kept.

All of it is stored only on your device: in your browser for the web version, or inside the app on your phone for the app version. Forge has no accounts and no server of its own.

What Forge never does

  • No tracking, no analytics and no advertising.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of any information.
  • No third-party code that collects data.
  • Nothing about you leaves your device unless you choose to send it.

When you choose to share

Some features create something you can send yourself: a share picture of a Forgeling, a backup file of your collection, or a text report. These go only where you put them, for example a message or your own files. Forge does not receive a copy.

Hosting

The web version of Forge is delivered by GitHub Pages. Like any website, GitHub receives basic technical details such as your IP address when it delivers the page, under GitHub's privacy statement. Forge itself never receives them. If you install Forge from an app store, that store's own privacy policy covers the download.

Keeping or deleting your data

Because your data is only on your device, you are in control of it. To delete everything, use "Erase everything on this device" in Forge's Settings, or delete the app (or clear this site's data in your browser). There is no other copy anywhere, so there is nothing for anyone to delete on a server.

Age

While Forge is being tested, it is for adults 18 and up. Forge is not directed at children.

Changes

If Forge ever changes how it handles data, for example by adding accounts or backups to a server, this policy will be updated first, and you will see the change in the app before anything new happens.

Contact

Questions or requests: see the support page.
