Common questions

Will I lose my Forgelings if I miss a day? Never. Missed days and rest days don't reset anything.

How do I move my collection to a new phone? In Settings, under Backup, save a copy. On the new phone, use Restore and choose that file.

Something looks wrong after an update. Close Forgelings completely and open it again. On iPhone, swipe it away in the app switcher first.

How do I delete everything? Settings, then "Erase everything on this device". Forgelings keeps no copy anywhere else.
